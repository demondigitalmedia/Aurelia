Throwing

Tap the disc for a clean, flat throw. AUTO power is set to just reach the basket.
Pull back and flick to shape it: the curve of your flick reads hyzer or anhyzer.
Hold the disc then slide: left and right tilt it, forward and back set the nose.
Tap the world to aim somewhere else. VIEW cycles behind you, overhead, above the trees and from the pin.

Sharing courses with the crew

  1. Build a hole, or put a course together under the Courses tab.
  2. In the library, tap a hole and choose Share hole file, or open a course and tap Share course file. You get a small .aurelia.json file.
  3. Send it however you like: text, Discord, email.
  4. Your friend opens the builder, taps Import and picks the file. The holes, and the course if there is one, land in their library to play or remix.

Your holes save in this browser on this device. Share files are also your backup, so export the ones you love.
